Research

Black hole perturbation theory and gravitational self-force, aimed at extreme- and intermediate-mass-ratio inspirals for LISA, in vacuum and inside the environments that surround real black holes.

  • Relativistic environments

    Massive black holes are often surrounded by accretion discs or, possibly, ultralight scalar clouds. I compute how a small body perturbs that environment, the wakes and spiral density waves it launches, and the forces and torques that act back on its orbit, all in the Kerr geometry and without a weak-field expansion.

  • 1PA waveforms

    An extreme-mass-ratio inspiral completes tens of thousands of orbits in LISA's band, so its phase has to be right to first post-adiabatic (1PA) order. I contribute to the second-order self-force programme, including the Kerr metric perturbations it is built from, and to precision modelling of the environmental effects (discs, scalar clouds) that enter the waveform at the same 1PA order.

  • Strong-field gravity

    Exact and near-exact results close to the horizon: closed-form plunging geodesics in Kerr, charged-particle motion and energy extraction around magnetic black holes, and lensing and wave optics in the strong field.
